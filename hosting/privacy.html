Green Pyramid Privacy Policy

Effective July 19, 2026

This policy describes how Green Pyramid ("the app"), published by Craig Glendenning, handles your information. Green Pyramid is a personal development app in which you organize life categories, track daily tasks, and receive AI-assisted coaching. The app does not require an account, and the personal content you create is stored on your device.

Information stored on your device

Your life categories, tasks, task completion history, vision statement, chat conversations, and settings are stored in a local database on your device. This data stays on your device: we operate no account system and no server-side database of user content. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Information processed by our AI features

When you use AI-powered features (the coach chat, generated motivations and quotes, task suggestions, vision statement help, and progress insights), the app sends the relevant content to our secure backend, which forwards it to OpenAI to generate a response. Depending on the feature, this content can include your chat messages, category and task names, task completion statistics, and text you provide such as your vision statement.

  • Our backend processes these requests transiently. It does not store your content and holds no database of it.
  • Requests are verified with Firebase App Check to confirm they come from a genuine copy of the app.
  • OpenAI processes the content as an API data processor under its API data usage policies, which state that API data is not used to train OpenAI models.

Do not include information in chats that you do not want processed by these services.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, which help keep the app and its AI features free. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), and information about your interactions with ads. The app does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework and does not request permission to track you across other companies' apps or websites; ads are served on a contextual basis. For details on how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics and diagnostics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate app usage, such as which screens are visited and general device characteristics. This helps us improve the app. Advertising and analytics SDKs may also collect crash logs and performance data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules daily reminders locally on your device. Notification scheduling does not send data off your device.

What we share, and what we do not

  • We share data with the service providers described above (Google for advertising, analytics, and app integrity; OpenAI for AI responses) only as needed to operate those features.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, property, or safety.

Data retention

Content you create lives on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Our backend retains no user content. Data collected by Google and OpenAI is retained under their respective policies linked above.

Your rights and choices

  • You can delete all app content at any time by deleting your data in the app or uninstalling it.
  • You can decline notification permission, or revoke it in system settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. Contact us at the address below and we will help.

Children's privacy

Green Pyramid is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will address it.

Security

Communication with our backend and third-party services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS), and backend access is gated by device attestation (Firebase App Check). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
