## Promotional copy

Green Pyramid helps you turn the life you care about into a calm, visible daily
practice. Shape a personal pyramid of categories, choose small actions, and
check in without losing sight of the bigger picture.

## Description

Build a daily practice around what matters to you.

Green Pyramid gives your priorities a simple, visual home. Create categories,
add practical habits, and check in with a tracker that stays useful even when
you are offline. Your history remains yours to review, so a missed day is
information—not a verdict.

When you choose the optional subscription, the Council of Advisors can help you
reflect on a category, and the app can turn your local activity into a gentle,
plain-language Analysis journey. The Newsfeed can also provide AI-written
reflection when the feature is available to your entitled account. AI guidance
is advisory; the tracker, saved habits and local check-ins remain usable without
it.

Green Pyramid is designed for steady attention rather than pressure: no
leaderboards, no public rankings and no claims of guaranteed outcomes.

## Subscription disclosure

An optional auto-renewing subscription unlocks AI-guided Council conversations,
the Analysis journey and eligible AI Newsfeed generation. The live price,
billing period, renewal terms and cancellation controls are shown by Apple on
the purchase sheet and in the app's subscription settings before purchase.
Users can restore eligible purchases through the app. Core tracking remains
available without the subscription.
