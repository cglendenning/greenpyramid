## Full description

Turn what matters to you into a daily practice.

Green Pyramid is a private, values-based habit tracker. Build a visual pyramid
of the areas you care about, choose small actions, and check in each day. Your
local tracker stays useful offline, and your history helps you notice patterns
without turning progress into a competition.

With the optional subscription, the Council of Advisors offers reflective,
AI-guided conversations. The Analysis journey turns your own recent check-ins
into six plain-language pages: showing up, strengths, rhythm, returning,
one gentle opportunity and an encouraging close. Eligible accounts can also
receive an AI-written Newsfeed reflection. These features are advisory and do
not replace professional advice.

Free tracker features include categories, habits, check-ins, saved history and
local editing. AI guidance is an optional paid surface and is never presented
as a guaranteed result.

## Subscription disclosure

The optional subscription is auto-renewing. Google Play shows the current
price, billing period, renewal terms and cancellation controls before purchase.
Users can restore eligible purchases in the app. The free tracker remains
available without an active subscription.
