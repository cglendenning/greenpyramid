# Green Pyramid privacy policy

**Effective date:** 2026-09-20

Public policy URL: https://greenpyramid-privacy.cglendenning.chatgpt.site

Green Pyramid stores the information needed to provide its tracker, account
linking, optional calendar context, notifications, subscriptions and optional
AI-guided features. The public, current version of this policy is hosted at
the URL above.

## Information used

The app may process account identifiers and provider information, pyramid
categories, habits, check-ins, explanations, notification state, subscription
entitlement and optional calendar availability. AI-guided features send the
minimum authenticated context needed for the requested response to the
Green Pyramid backend and its configured AI provider.

## How it is used

The data is used to save and restore the user's pyramid, operate the tracker,
schedule requested reminders, maintain account ownership, enforce entitlement
and spend limits, and provide requested AI guidance. Green Pyramid does not
sell personal information or use public leaderboards.

## Choices and retention

Calendar and notification access are optional. Tracker data remains available
locally without network access where the feature supports offline use. Account
linked content is retained while the account exists, subject to the final
account-deletion process published with this policy.

## Security

Authenticated backend requests use Firebase authentication and App Check where
required. Billing and trial authority are verified by the server and store
provider; the client is not the authority for paid access.

## Contact and deletion

You can permanently delete your account in the app: Settings → Account →
Delete account. The app deletes the Firebase account, account-scoped cloud
records, local tracker data, the local profile photo and pending reminders.
Deletion cannot be undone. Apple and Google subscriptions are managed by the
store and are not cancelled by deleting an account; cancel them separately in
the relevant store settings to prevent renewal. If you cannot open the app,
contact the publisher through the support channel shown on the public policy
page so the deletion request can be verified and processed.
